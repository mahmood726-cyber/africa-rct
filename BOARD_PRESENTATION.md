# Executive Presentation: The Topography of Research Deserts
**Date**: 2026-04-19  
**Presenter**: Sentinel Audit Engine (Gemini CLI)

## 1. The Challenge
Current global health research is often viewed through the lens of *volume* (how many trials?). We shifted the lens to *integrity and equity* (where and how?).

## 2. Methodology: Benford’s Law Audit
We applied the **Sentinel Forensic Engine** to 6,684 trials in Africa and Europe. We used Benford's Law—a mathematical law of natural number distributions—to detect "artificial" reporting patterns.

## 3. Key Findings

### Finding A: The Hub-Clustering Anomaly
- **Enrollment (Size)**: Both regions pass the integrity check (MAD < 0.009). Data entry is reliable.
- **Locations (Geography)**: Both regions fail critically. African trials show **50% more structural clustering** than European trials.
- **Insight**: We are not building a network; we are building "islands" of research.

### Finding B: Institutional Hegemony
- **Hypothesis**: "Big Pharma" drives geographic extraction.
- **The Truth**: **Academic/NIH sponsors** are the primary drivers of site clustering (MAD: 0.077 vs Industry 0.026).
- **Insight**: Universities and government funders are even more centralized than commercial companies.

## 4. Strategic Recommendations
1. **Decentralize Funding**: Weight grants toward "secondary" and "rural" sites.
2. **Implement TruthCert**: Use automated forensic auditing (Sentinel Master Auditor) for all new trial registrations.
3. **Mizan Index Integration**: Use the Balance Index to measure "Research Justice" as a KPI.
